# Code of Conduct

Aftertime brings together people from different disciplines, countries and
political perspectives around a shared physical reality.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise and open to correction.
- Critique claims and methods, not people.
- Distinguish evidence, inference, uncertainty and opinion.
- Welcome newcomers and explain specialized terminology.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, security and lawful data-use restrictions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate deception, doxxing, disclosure
of confidential data, political intimidation and sustained disruption are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Until a community team and private reporting channel are established, report
conduct concerns to repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, pause interactions or restrict participation when necessary to protect
people and the project. Enforcement decisions should be documented as
transparently as privacy and safety permit.

This provisional policy will be replaced with a ratified multilingual code of
conduct as part of the foundation roadmap.
