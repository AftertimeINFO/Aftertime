## Two layers of reality

Humanity coordinates companies, states and economies through money, securities,
laws and borders. These abstractions are not meaningless: they shape behavior,
allocate power and determine access to resources. But they are created by people
and remain changeable through human agreement.

Beneath them lies a physical reality that does not depend on recognition or
belief: life, matter, energy, space and time. Matter crosses borders regardless
of how maps are drawn. Energy conversions follow physical laws regardless of
their financial treatment. A political decision may change who controls a
resource, but it cannot change the conservation laws governing that resource.

Economic systems therefore matter, but they are not the foundation on which life
depends. A financial balance shows how society assigns and distributes value.
Aftertime should show what physically happens to the world as a result of that
distribution.

Financial accounts can report growth while concentrated resources are depleted,
materials are dispersed, ecosystems are damaged and recoverable energy is lost.
A civilization cannot manage what it cannot see.

Aftertime proposes a planetary accounting system for matter and energy. Like a
management balance, it begins with opening stocks and ends with closing stocks.
Unlike a financial balance, its primary units describe physical reality.

Aftertime is not intended to replace economics, law or politics. It adds the
missing physical layer against which their real-world consequences can be tested.

Matter does not appear or disappear in ordinary processes; it changes location,
composition and form. Crude oil becomes fuels, polymers, carbon dioxide, water,
heat and waste. Ore becomes metal, infrastructure, corrosion products and slag.
Lithium moves from deposits into batteries and eventually into recycled feedstock
or dispersed waste. Each step changes what remains possible in the future.

## Stocks, flows and transformations

Aftertime represents the planet through:

1. **Stocks** — matter in a defined place, time and state.
2. **Flows** — movement between stocks through natural or human systems.
3. **Transformations** — processes that change composition, form or usability.

Every quantity must be connected to time, geography, a material identity, a
measurement method, provenance and uncertainty.

## Matter cannot be separated from energy

A material may still exist but become so diluted, contaminated or geographically
dispersed that recovering it is impractical. Availability therefore depends on
concentration, technology and energy, not only on mass.

The energy balance records sources, conversions, useful work, storage and
dissipation. Together, the two balances measure not simply how much exists, but
how much usable possibility humanity retains.

## Physical and political reality

A resource has a location and a control context. Legal ownership, economic
influence, political authority, military control and practical access may belong
to different actors. Aftertime must preserve these distinctions rather than
compressing them into a single owner field.

This makes it possible to study concentration of power, strategic dependencies,
supply-chain fragility and unequal access without confusing observation with
political judgment.

## From records to foresight

The system should eventually answer:

- How much of a resource is accessible, and for how long?
- Which materials and energy pathways are becoming critical?
- Where are irreversible or extremely costly transformations occurring?
- Which flows are vulnerable to disruption or political concentration?
- What recovery energy and infrastructure will future generations require?
- Which decisions preserve the greatest range of future options?

The goal is not to stop transformation. Life itself is transformation. The goal
is to make the consequences measurable, auditable and visible early enough to
act.

## Life is not guaranteed

Life is a rare and fragile organization of matter. The universe does not
guarantee its continuation and is indifferent to humanity's survival. Preserving
life requires more than good intentions: it requires the ability to understand
physical limits, recognize long-term consequences and use matter and energy with
care and efficiency.

Aftertime is founded on a sober but hopeful premise: humanity can make better
decisions when it can see what its decisions physically do to the world.

## Mission

**Aftertime helps humanity understand how matter and energy move and transform,
so that decisions made today preserve the possibility of life tomorrow.**
