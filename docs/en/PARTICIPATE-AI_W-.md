# Participate

[en](PARTICIPATE.md) · [uk](../uk/PARTICIPATE.md) · [home](../../README.md)

Aftertime is larger than a software project. Contributions may be scientific,
technical, institutional, financial or communicative.

## Ways to contribute

### Researchers and domain experts

Review assumptions, identify standards, propose conservation equations, evaluate
uncertainty, recommend datasets or design an auditable pilot.

### Engineers and data practitioners

Work on ontologies, provenance, ingestion, reconciliation, geospatial systems,
APIs, visualization, simulations, testing and reproducible infrastructure.

### Data providers and institutions

Offer lawful access to datasets, metadata and expertise. Help define validation
agreements that preserve independence and permit publication of limitations.

### Partners and funders

Support bounded public deliverables: a scientific review, dataset inventory,
pilot balance, open-source component, fellowship or community program. Funding
must not buy control over scientific conclusions or suppress inconvenient results.

### Writers, designers and translators

Make the idea understandable without overstating current capabilities. Improve
documentation, terminology, accessibility and language coverage.

## A good first contribution

Open an Issue or Discussion containing:

1. the problem or opportunity;
2. why it matters to the mission;
3. evidence, sources or relevant expertise;
4. a bounded proposed outcome;
5. known legal, ethical or data constraints;
6. how the result could be independently checked.

Small, verifiable contributions are preferred over broad promises.

## Contribution principles

- Distinguish measurements, estimates, assumptions and opinions.
- Cite primary sources and preserve provenance.
- State uncertainty and known limitations.
- Do not upload confidential, personal, restricted or unlawfully obtained data.
- Disclose relevant financial or institutional conflicts of interest.
- Respect people across disciplines, countries and political perspectives.
- Optimize for humanity's shared physical reality, not a sponsor's preferred story.

## For investors

Aftertime may create commercial opportunities around services, integration,
analysis and decision support, but the core public methodology must remain
inspectable. Investors interested in patient, mission-aligned infrastructure are
invited to propose transparent funding structures with clear public outcomes.

## Contact

Until dedicated channels are established, use GitHub Issues for bounded tasks and
GitHub Discussions for ideas, partnerships and scientific debate.
